
MODEL SUMMARY
============================================================

Model:
Isolation Forest

Purpose:
Unsupervised behavioral anomaly detection.

Unit of analysis:
User-day

Behavioral features:
- Login count
- Logout count
- After-hours login
- Unique PCs
- Work duration
- USB connections
- USB disconnections
- After-hours USB
- Web activity
- Unique domains
- After-hours web activity
- Personal anomaly strength
- Role anomaly strength
- Behavioral deviation score

Risk calibration:
70% ML anomaly risk
30% behavioral deviation percentile

Risk thresholds:
0-39     Low
40-69    Medium
70-89    High
90-100   Critical

Investigation priorities:
P1: Risk >= 90
P2: Risk >= 70
P3: Risk >= 40
P4: Risk < 40

Important:
This is an unsupervised anomaly-detection system.
Anomaly flags and risk scores are not ground-truth labels.
